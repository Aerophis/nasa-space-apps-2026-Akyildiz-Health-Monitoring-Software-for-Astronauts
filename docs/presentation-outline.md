## Presenter guidance

- Spend most of the walkthrough on slides 3–5 and working interactions.
- Use plain English: explain “synthetic” as fictional demo records and “serum” as
  the liquid part of a blood sample used for laboratory testing.
- Describe coverage as available measurements, not a health score or treatment goal.
- Never imply live astronaut monitoring, NASA endorsement, clinical validation,
  an integrated AI inference model, or confirmed official Resources compliance.
- Credit the team leader's direction, verified roles, and Codex assistance. Do not
  invent individual completed work or scientific acceptance.
- Use the [submission draft](submission-draft.md), [demo storyboard](demo-storyboard.md),
  and [Sprint 4 report](sprint-4.md) as the factual source for the final deck.
